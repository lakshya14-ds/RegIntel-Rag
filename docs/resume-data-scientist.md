## 💼 Project Entry: Data Scientist / Applied NLP Specialist

### **RegIntel RAG — Regulatory Intelligence & Change Analysis System**
*Python | Information Retrieval | Sentence Transformers | MLflow | NLP | Evaluation Metrics | MongoDB Atlas*

- **Systematic Retrieval Optimization**: Formulated and benchmarked dense vector search, lexical BM25Plus, and cross-encoder neural rerankers across 50+ annotated regulatory scenarios; achieved **92.0% Recall@5** and **0.84 MRR**, systematically logged and compared via MLflow experiment tracking.
- **Temporal & Legal Supersession Modeling**: Developed a deterministic temporal ranking and regex-driven supersession graph to resolve document version chronology (`CURRENT` vs. `HISTORICAL`), preventing outdated regulatory circulars from outranking recent Master Circulars.
- **Grounded Generation & Quality Metrics**: Created automated evaluation pipelines measuring Grounded Claim Rate (**4.92 / 5.0**), Answer Relevance (**4.85 / 5.0**), Citation Support Rate, and Safe Abstention on unanswerable financial regulatory inquiries.
- **Structure-Aware Document Processing**: Built a domain-specific cleaning and tokenization pipeline preserving hierarchical headings, section numbers, and tabular clauses across complex financial PDF/HTML documents with SHA-256 deduplication.
